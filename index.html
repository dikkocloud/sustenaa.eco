Mohammad Abubakar

CEO

Muhammad Abubakar is a chemist, and climate educator. He is an M.Sc. candidate in Analytical and Environmental Chemistry at Gombe State University, where his research focuses on biochar production from agricultural residues for soil carbon sequestration and land restoration in Gombe. He serves as CEO at Sustenaa, where his work spans climate education, renewable energy advocacy, and plastic waste awareness across Northeast Nigeria. Muhammad blends technical chemistry expertise with grassroots environmental advocacy, reaching a growing community of followers through his digital platforms. He is also a poet, a bibliophile, bringing a love of language and storytelling into his climate work..
