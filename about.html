About Sustenaa

Sustainability taught well and built well.

Most eco-education stops at awareness. Most sustainable computing stops at a pledge. Sustenaa sits at the place those two usually miss each other — teaching people how systems work, and building the systems so the teaching holds up.

Education

We design eco-literacy that's practical rather than performative — the kind someone can actually act on the same week they learn it. That means plain language over jargon, real numbers over vague claims, and lessons built around the decisions people already make: what they buy, build, and switch off.

Computing

The tools we build carry the same standard. We choose efficient architecture and lean infrastructure as a default, not a retrofit — because software that wastes less compute is easier to trust, and easier to teach from.

What we hold to

A few things we don't compromise on.

Clarity over hype

We'd rather explain something simply than make it sound impressive.

Built, not bolted on

Sustainability is a design constraint from day one, not a feature added later.

Open to scrutiny

If we can't show our work, we don't expect anyone to trust our claims.
